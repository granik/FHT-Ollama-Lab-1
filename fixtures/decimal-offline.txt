Decimal("10.005") preserves the decimal input. Binary floating-point conversion
can introduce unwanted digits. quantize(Decimal("0.01"), rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP)
rounds to cents; a tie rounds away from zero. The default decimal context uses
ROUND_HALF_EVEN, which can produce different answers at ties.
The business rules, not the documentation, define WHEN each rounding happens.
